# Privacy Policy

**Zoned In** (the "Bot")
Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy explains what information the Bot collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the Bot you agree to this policy.

**Operator:** [Nocturne]
**Contact:** [https://discord.gg/PZmPcNks3J]

## 1. Information we collect

The Bot stores only the following:

| Data | Why it's stored |
| --- | --- |
| Your Discord user ID | To link your saved timezone to your account. |
| Your timezone (for example `Asia/Manila` or `UTC+08:00`) | To convert times you mention and to answer `/timefor`. |
| A server's Discord ID | To store that server's settings. |
| A server's chosen admin role ID and auto-conversion on/off setting | To apply the server's configuration. |

Your timezone is set by you (or by a server admin on your behalf) and is shared across all servers where the Bot is present.

## 2. Messages

To detect times such as "6pm", the Bot reads the content of messages in servers it has been added to. Message content is processed in memory in real time to find times and is **not stored, logged, sold or shared**. Discord delivers server messages to the Bot, but it only analyzes messages from users who have saved a timezone, and it ignores direct messages.

## 3. Information we do not collect

We do not collect or store message content, usernames, avatars, email addresses, IP addresses, locations other than the timezone you provide, or any payment information.

## 4. How we use information

We use the stored data only to provide the Bot's features: converting times, answering timezone lookups, and applying server settings. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or any unrelated purpose.

## 5. Who can see your timezone

Your timezone is visible to other users through the Bot's replies and commands. For example, the Bot shows your UTC offset when it converts a time you mention, and anyone in a server with the Bot can use `/timefor` on you to see your current local time. Only set a timezone you are comfortable sharing.

Server admins, and members of a server's configured admin role, can set or remove your timezone.

## 6. Storage and security

Data is stored in a database on the server where the Bot is hosted. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no system is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security. The Bot's access token is kept private and is never included in the stored data.

## 7. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties. The Bot runs on Discord, so your use of it is also subject to [Discord's Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy). We may disclose information if required by law.

## 8. Retention and deletion

- Your timezone is kept until you delete it with `/removetimezone` (or an admin removes it).
- Server settings are kept while the Bot remains in the server.
- You can ask us to delete all data tied to your user ID, or a server's settings, by contacting us at the address above. We will act on valid requests within a reasonable time.

## 9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. You can do most of this yourself using the Bot's commands, or contact us for anything else.

## 10. Children

Discord requires users to be at least 13 years old (or the minimum age in their country). The Bot is not intended for anyone below that age and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used the Bot, contact us and we will delete the data.

## 11. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date shows the latest revision. Continued use of the Bot after changes means you accept the updated policy.
